# Ventilla

Bring <cg>live radio</c> to your Geometry Dash experience!

<cl>Ventilla</c> replaces the default main menu music with a **non-profit** live radio station made for the Geometry Dash community.

Radio URL: [radio.5infin.es](https://radio.5infin.es)
